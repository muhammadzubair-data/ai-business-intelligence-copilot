# Halden Supply Co. — Company Overview

## What we sell
Halden Supply Co. sells workspace products to businesses and consumers. The catalog has about 800 active SKUs in six product categories, grouped into three business units. Workspace covers Office Furniture and Ergonomics. Technology covers Tech Accessories and Print & Imaging. Consumables covers Office Supplies and Breakroom & Janitorial.

## Customer segments
Customers are classified into four segments when their account is created. Enterprise accounts are large organisations with negotiated pricing and a named account team. Mid-Market accounts are growing companies with a mix of direct and self-serve buying. SMB accounts are small businesses buying mostly online. Consumer accounts are individual shoppers. Segment does not change over the life of an account.

## Key accounts
Roughly the largest eight percent of Enterprise customers are designated key accounts. Key accounts have quarterly business reviews, and a key account that stops ordering is escalated to the regional sales director.

## Regions and fulfilment
We operate in four regions: North America, Europe, APAC and LATAM. Orders ship from five distribution centres: Chicago DC and Reno DC serve North America, Rotterdam DC serves Europe, Singapore DC serves APAC, and Sao Paulo DC serves LATAM.

## Sales channels
Customers buy through Direct Sales (account teams), the Web Store, third-party Marketplaces and Partner resellers. Marketplace orders carry a commission of about 15 percent, Partner orders about 10 percent, and Web Store orders a 2 percent payment fee. Direct Sales orders carry no channel fee.

## Sales teams
Direct Sales is organised into seven teams: NA Enterprise East, NA Enterprise West, NA Commercial, EU Enterprise, EU Commercial, APAC Sales and LATAM Sales. North American enterprise accounts are split between the East and West teams.
