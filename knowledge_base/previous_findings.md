# Previous Analytical Findings

These are summaries of earlier analyses by the analytics team. They describe patterns that were investigated and explained, so the same patterns should not be reported as new anomalies without new evidence.

## FIND-2023-03 — January dip is seasonal
Analysis of 2022 and 2023 showed that revenue falls every January compared with December, typically by 20 to 30 percent, as year-end budget spending ends and holiday promotions stop. When compared with the same month of the prior year, January performs in line with the annual trend. Month-over-month comparisons from December to January should not be treated as anomalies.

## FIND-2023-07 — Q4 discount depth is planned
Discount rates rise in November and December because of pre-approved seasonal promotions for SMB and Consumer customers. A higher Q4 discount rate is expected; the question to ask is whether it exceeded the promotion plan.

## FIND-2023-11 — Marketplace orders carry lower contribution margin
Marketplace commissions of about 15 percent mean that a shift of sales from Direct or Web to Marketplace lowers contribution margin even when revenue and gross margin are unchanged. Channel mix should be checked whenever contribution margin moves without a change in pricing or costs.

## FIND-2024-02 — Weekend orders are mostly Consumer
B2B customers rarely order at weekends, so weekday and weekend volumes should not be compared directly for B2B segments.

## FIND-2024-05 — Web order counts differ from the order system
Web orders from the analytics tool typically differ from order-system counts by several percent because of ad blockers, consent choices and tag timing. Use the order system for business reporting.
