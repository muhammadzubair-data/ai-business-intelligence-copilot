# Product and Merchandising Notes

## Category overview
Office Furniture includes desks, chairs, storage and meeting tables; it has the highest price points and the lowest unit volumes. Ergonomics covers monitor arms, footrests, keyboard trays, lumbar supports and anti-fatigue mats. Tech Accessories covers docking stations, keyboards, mice, headsets, webcams and adapters. Print & Imaging covers printers, toner, ink and scanners. Office Supplies and Breakroom & Janitorial are high-volume, low-price consumables that customers reorder frequently.

## Pricing
List prices are reviewed every January. Category-level price changes outside the January review are recorded in product_price_history with their effective date.

## Supplier costs
Supplier unit costs are stored in product_cost_history with effective dates. Each product has one primary supplier. Cost changes flow into cost of goods sold for orders placed on or after the effective date.

## New product launches
The Halden AeroDesk Pro Standing Desk launched on 2 September 2024 as the flagship of the Office Furniture range, supported by a launch campaign across all regions. New products appear in the product dimension with their launch date and cannot be sold before it.

## Seasonality
Demand is seasonal. Q4 is the strongest quarter, driven by year-end budget spending in B2B segments and holiday promotions for SMB and Consumer customers, including Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Discounts are deeper in November and December. January is consistently the weakest month.
